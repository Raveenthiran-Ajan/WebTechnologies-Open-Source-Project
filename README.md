# About The Project

The School Management System is a robust, full-featured web application built on the MERN (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js) stack. It is designed to digitize and simplify the complexities of school administration and academic management. 

This system provides a centralized platform for administrators, teachers, parents, and students, ensuring seamless information flow and effortless communication. From attendance tracking and assignment submission to parent-teacher communication, this project covers the essential needs of a modern educational institution.

# Key Features

- **Role-Based Access Control:** The system supports four distinct user roles—Admin, Teacher, Parent, and Student—each with a tailored dashboard and permissions to ensure that users can only access relevant information and functionalities.

- **Multi-Language Support:** To cater to a diverse user base, the application is equipped with multi-language capabilities, allowing users to switch between languages like English, Tamil, and Sinhala for a more inclusive experience.

- **Academic Management:** Effortlessly manage classes, subjects, and assignments. Teachers can upload assignments and grade submissions, while students and parents can track academic performance and view grades.

- **Attendance & Leave Management:** Teachers can efficiently take attendance, which is then accessible to students and parents in real-time. Parents can also submit leave requests directly through the portal.

- **Communication Hub:** A built-in notice system allows administrators to broadcast announcements to all users. Additionally, a complaint management feature facilitates direct and accountable communication between parents, students, and teachers.

- **Responsive Design:** The user interface is designed to be responsive, providing a seamless experience across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

# User Roles & Functionalities

### 👨‍💼 Admin
- **Centralized Dashboard:** A comprehensive overview of the entire school's operations.
- **User Management:** Add, edit, and manage student, teacher, and parent accounts.
- **Academic Setup:** Create new classes and subjects and assign teachers accordingly.
- **Broadcast Notices:** Publish school-wide announcements and important updates.
- **Handle Complaints:** View and address complaints submitted by other users.

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher
- **Class & Student Management:** View assigned classes and manage student information.
- **Attendance Tracking:** Take and update daily attendance for their classes.
- **Assignment Workflow:** Upload new assignments, view student submissions, and provide marks.
- **Performance Monitoring:** Track the academic progress of their students.

### 👨‍🎓 Student
- **Personalized Dashboard:** Access a summary of grades, attendance, and upcoming assignments.
- **Subject & Grade Viewing:** Check marks and performance in all enrolled subjects.
- **Assignment Submission:** View and submit assignments online.
- **Stay Informed:** Receive and view notices from the school administration.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent
- **Child Monitoring:** A dedicated portal to track their child's academic journey.
- **Academic & Attendance Reports:** View detailed reports on their child's grades and attendance history.
- **Leave Requests:** Submit leave requests directly to the school for approval.
- **Stay Connected:** Receive school notices and communicate concerns through the complaint system.
